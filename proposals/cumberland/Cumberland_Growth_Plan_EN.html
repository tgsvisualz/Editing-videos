Who we're talking to

Three future students, three reasons to start, and one program that fits each life.

Career changer
THE CAREER CHANGER

28 to 45, working full time, stuck in a job with no future. Wants a real skill and a new title without quitting: evenings, online, with funding to make it possible.

Best approach: Facebook and Instagram, "Change careers, not your life", evening schedules, funding explained, the info session.
The starter
THE STARTER

18 to 25, out of CEGEP or university, lives on TikTok and Instagram. Wants something practical that leads to a job fast, not four more years of theory.

Best approach: the 8 short videos a month on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook Reels, boosted to 18–24s: "Learn this in 30 seconds", student life, "16 months to a new job title."
The newcomer
THE NEWCOMER

New to Canada, with experience but no Canadian credential. Wants a recognized diploma, in French or English, and a way into the local job market.

Best approach: Facebook and Instagram, info sessions in both languages, graduates who did it, "A Canadian diploma in 16 months."
What matters to them
  • A job at the end, not just a certificate
  • A schedule that fits work and family
  • Knowing how to pay for it before they apply
  • Teachers who actually do the job
